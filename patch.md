# 1.10.1
## General
__Creeps__
> - Gold from all creeps reduced by ~12%
__UI__
> - Added a new pop-up window with hero data when hovering over the portrait.
__Attributes__
> - Intelligence now gives Spell Amp: 0.02% per point
> - Strength now reduces incoming damage: 30% * str / (str + 2500) (1000 str = 8.6%, 2500 = 15%, 5000 = 20%, never reaches 30%)
__Summons__
> - Added: all player summons now scale from their owner's attributes
> - Agility: +15% of owner's armor, +15% of owner's bonus attack speed, +0.5 move speed per agility, 0.1% armor ignore per agility (max 50%)
> - Intelligence: +15% of owner's attack damage
> - Strength: +15% of owner's max HP, 0.05% damage resist per strength (max 30%)
__Tormentor__
> - Reworked
